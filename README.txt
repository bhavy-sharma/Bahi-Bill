Bahi Bill - simple GST bill maker
=================================
1. Unzip the folder and double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). No internet needed.
2. Step 1: fill your shop details once and click "Save shop details".
3. Step 2: customer name (leave blank for cash bill).  Step 3: items, quantity, rate, GST %.
4. Click Print, or PDF to save the bill as a PDF file.
5. Click "New bill" for the next customer. The bill number goes up by itself.
Items you bill are remembered, so next time the rate and GST fill in automatically.
Payment QR: at the bottom of the form. Make it from your UPI ID (amount fills in by itself) or upload
your own PhonePe / Google Pay / Paytm QR image. You can change heading, note, size and colour, or hide it.
